Count as a day outside the UK
The continuous residence guidance counts whole days only: “part day absences, less than 24 hours are not counted”. You are in the UK for part of the day you depart and part of the day you arrive, so neither is an absence and both start unticked. Tick a box to plan against the stricter reading of that travel day. Data lives in trips.js.
