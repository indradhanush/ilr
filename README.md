# ILR: Indefinite Leave to Remain [![codecov](https://codecov.io/github/indradhanush/ilr/graph/badge.svg?token=MTIAVCPLNH)](https://codecov.io/github/indradhanush/ilr)
[https://igupta.in/ilr](https://igupta.in/ilr)

Tracking days spent outside the UK. Free to use. Yours to keep. Forever.

## Data retention, or the lack of it

Everything you enter stays in your browser. Trips are saved to your browser's
localStorage so they are still there when you come back. This is a static
webpage and does not track or store any of your data or analytics. And it never
will.

Your data lives in your browser until you press `Reset` or clear the site's
data.

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses like any web
host. That is covered by [GitHub's privacy
statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Disclaimer

This is a planning tool, not immigration advice. The rules are modelled from the
Home Office continuous residence guidance, but only the Home Office decides your
application. Check the [official
guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/continuous-residence-caseworker-guidance)
and get professional advice if you need.
